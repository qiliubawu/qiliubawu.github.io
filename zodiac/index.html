兽十二兽图鉴

古人称十二生肖为「十二兽」「十二神」，与十二地支一一相配。悬停每张卡片，翻转可见其起源典故与所主时辰。

01 / 12子 鼠 Rat ZI · 23:00–01:00 Water · Yang
鼠
Rat · Midnight
Zi hour · 23:00 – 01:00

First of the twelve: the Rat rode the Ox's back, then leapt ahead at the finish. Midnight is when rats are busiest, hence the pairing. On palace wares it wishes many heirs and a full granary.

Quick-wittedNimbleStrategicProsperous
02 / 12丑 牛 Ox CHOU · 01:00–03:00 Earth · Yin
牛
Ox · Cockcrow
Chou hour · 01:00 – 03:00

The Ox is the root of the farm, offered as the great sacrifice. At the Chou hour farmers rise to feed it, when it chews the cud most diligently. The ox-plough stood for a settled realm.

DiligentSteadfastEnduringReliable
03 / 12寅 虎 Tiger YIN · 03:00–05:00 Wood · Yang
虎
Tiger · Daybreak
Yin hour · 03:00 – 05:00

Yin is the hinge of night and day, when beasts are fiercest and the tiger's roar fills the hills. Tiger tallies once moved armies; in folk custom the tiger guards the house and wards off evil.

FierceBoldProtectiveCommanding
04 / 12卯 兔 Rabbit MAO · 05:00–07:00 Wood · Yin
兔
Rabbit · Sunrise
Mao hour · 05:00 – 07:00

At Mao the sun rises in the east, and the jade rabbit pounds elixir on the moon. Rabbits bearing lingzhi on Ming porcelain are omens of long life; Mao means grass breaking through.

GentleAgileKindLong-lived
05 / 12辰 龙 Dragon CHEN · 07:00–09:00 Earth · Yang
龙
Dragon · Morning
Chen hour · 07:00 – 09:00

At Chen mist gathers and rain is summoned, and the dragon shows between the clouds. It stood for the emperor himself — the Nine-Dragon Wall is its highest form.

NobleAspiringShiftingAuspicious
06 / 12巳 蛇 Snake SI · 09:00–11:00 Fire · Yin
蛇
Snake · Forenoon
Si hour · 09:00 – 11:00

By Si the mist has burned off and snakes leave their burrows to hunt. In remotest antiquity the snake was the dragon's prototype — Fuxi and Nüwa have serpent bodies.

SuppleWisePatientWatchful
07 / 12午 马 Horse WU · 11:00–13:00 Fire · Yang
马
Horse · Noon
Wu hour · 11:00 – 13:00

At noon the sun stands overhead; the horse is fiery and of yang, and so is paired with Wu. The Six Steeds of Zhaoling are its great monument. It carries the wish 'success on arrival'.

SpiritedStrongFar-goingHonoured
08 / 12未 羊 Goat WEI · 13:00–15:00 Earth · Yin
羊
Goat · Past Noon
Wei hour · 13:00 – 15:00

By Wei the sun leans west and the pasture is rich — the hour for grazing. Yang (goat) sounds like xiang (auspicious); Han tile-ends bear goat motifs, and 'three yang' puns on sunshine.

MildHarmoniousCourteousBlessed
09 / 12申 猴 Monkey SHEN · 15:00–17:00 Metal · Yang
猴
Monkey · Teatime
Shen hour · 15:00 – 17:00

At Shen the sun sets in the western hills and monkeys call as they return to roost. Hou (monkey) puns on hou (marquis): a monkey riding a deer means 'sealed as marquis at once'.

CleverAdaptableLivelyFavoured
10 / 12酉 鸡 Rooster YOU · 17:00–19:00 Metal · Yin
鸡
Rooster · Sunset
You hour · 17:00 – 19:00

At You night falls and the chickens go to roost. The rooster holds five virtues — refinement, martial bearing, courage, benevolence, trust. Ji (rooster) puns on ji (fortune).

PunctualFaithfulValiantFortunate
11 / 12戌 狗 Dog XU · 19:00–21:00 Earth · Yang
狗
Dog · Dusk
Xu hour · 19:00 – 21:00

At Xu dusk closes in and the dog guards the gate, more alert than at any hour. It is the watchkeeper of the Six Domestic Animals; the Book of Songs asks that 'the shaggy dog not bark'.

LoyalAlertTrustyGuarding
12 / 12亥 猪 Pig HAI · 21:00–23:00 Water · Yin
猪
Pig · Rest
Hai hour · 21:00 – 23:00

At Hai the night is deep and still, and the pig sleeps most soundly of all. It heads the Six Domestic Animals and stands for abundance; Han tombs hold a jade pig in the hand — 'wealth in hand'.

GenialContentAbundantSerene
时十二时辰长卷

古人以十二地支纪一日之时，每一时辰配一生肖、应一事象。横向拖拽展卷，可览自子至亥的昼夜流转。

子鼠
夜半

天地混沌初开，鼠于暗处最活跃。古人此时安寝，以待明日。

01:00 – 03:00

鸡鸣

农家起身喂牛，牛反刍最勤。鸡鸣之声渐起，长夜将阑。

15:00 – 17:00

17:00 – 19:00

19:00 – 21:00

黄昏

暮色四合，犬守门护院、警惕最甚。人定之前，闭户安歇。

21:00 – 23:00

人定

夜深人静，猪睡得最沉。万籁俱寂，一昼夜至此圆满。

艺非遗生肖艺术展厅

剪纸、年画、邮票、青铜兽首——生肖作为民俗母题，在各类非遗技艺中流转千年。悬停展板即可展开细览。

剪
剪纸 · 窗花
剪纸 · 十二生肖窗花

以红纸为底、剪刻成生肖形象，贴于窗棂取「迎春纳福」之意。陕北、蔚县剪纸尤以生肖题材见长，线条镂空、透光成影，是春节最鲜活的民俗符号。

国家级非遗
画
年画 · 门神
年画 · 生肖门神

杨柳青、桃花坞年画常以生肖入图，套色木版印刷，色彩浓艳。鼠娶亲、猴献桃等题材诙谐有趣，寄托岁首祈福、驱邪迎祥的朴素愿望。

木版套印
邮
邮票 · 方寸
邮票 · 庚申首轮生肖票

1980 年庚申猴票为中国首枚生肖邮票，黄永玉绘制，此后年年发行、成系列传承。方寸之间融国画、雕刻、印刷三艺，被誉「国家名片」。

生肖系列
铜
青铜 · 兽首
青铜 · 圆明园兽首

清乾隆年间海晏堂十二生肖水力钟喷泉，十二兽首人身为铜铸，按时辰轮流喷水报时。后流散海外，其中七尊已归国，成为文物流失与回归的时代见证。

海晏堂遗珍
瓷
瓷器 · 生肖
瓷器 · 生肖俑与青花

唐代十二生肖俑为镇墓明器，兽首人身、著袍执笏，按地支方位列置。明清青花、粉彩亦多绘生肖纹样，与吉祥花卉组合成「岁岁平安」图式。
